Battleship looks like a game of pure guessing, but good players sink fleets in far fewer shots than beginners. Here's how to play, and how to aim smarter.

## What you need

Each player has two **10 × 10 grids**. Columns are marked **A to J** and rows **1 to 10**. The lower grid holds your own fleet. On the upper grid you record your shots at the enemy. You can play with the plastic board game or with pencil and squared paper.

## The fleet

Each player places five ships:

| Ship | Length |
| --- | --- |
| Carrier | 5 squares |
| Battleship | 4 squares |
| Cruiser | 3 squares |
| Submarine | 3 squares |
| Destroyer | 2 squares |

That's **17 squares** of ships in total. Ships go horizontally or vertically, never diagonally, and they can't overlap.

## How to play

1. Both players secretly place their ships.
2. Take turns calling out one coordinate, such as "C7".
3. Your opponent says **"hit"** or **"miss"**. Mark it on your tracking grid: usually red for a hit and white for a miss.
4. When every square of a ship has been hit, its owner announces **"You sank my battleship!"** (or whichever ship it was).
5. The first player to sink the whole enemy fleet wins.

A popular variation, sometimes called **salvo**, gives each player one shot for every ship they have left, fired all at once.

## Where to hide your ships

- **Spread them out.** Ships packed together let your opponent find several at once.
- **Don't make patterns.** Experienced players notice if you always use the edges or always the centre. Mix it up between games.
- **Avoid touching ships.** When ships sit side by side, a single "hit" can lead your opponent straight into the next one.
- **Hide the destroyer well.** It's the smallest ship, so it's the hardest to find and often the last one standing.

## How to find ships faster

### Hunt with a checkerboard pattern

The smallest ship is two squares long, so every ship must cover at least one square of a **checkerboard pattern**. Fire only at squares of one colour on an imaginary checkerboard (for example, A1, A3, B2, B4 and so on). You'll find every ship while using only half the shots.

### Switch to target mode after a hit

When you score a hit, fire at the four squares around it (up, down, left, right). Once you get a second hit, you know which way the ship runs. Keep firing along that line until it sinks, then go back to hunting.

### Think about which ships are left

Once the destroyer is sunk, the smallest remaining ship is three squares long. Spread your hunting shots further apart, every third square, to save shots.

### Aim at the middle early

A long ship is more likely to cover squares near the middle of the board than in the corners, because there are more ways to fit it there. Early hunting shots in the central area find the carrier and battleship more often.

## Quick tips

- Keep careful track of your shots so you never fire at the same square twice.
- After sinking a ship, check whether nearby hits belong to another ship.
- Don't forget to watch what your opponent is doing, too. If they're using a pattern, move your ships next game.
