The dice decide where you land, but you decide what to buy, when to build and which deals to accept. Over a long game those decisions matter far more than luck. Here are ten habits that win games.

## 1. Buy almost everything early

In the first few trips around the board, buy nearly every property you land on. Even streets you'll never build on are valuable as **trading chips**, and every street you own is one that nobody else can complete a set with. If you play with the official [auction rule](/official-monopoly-rules-explained), keep enough cash to win bargains when others pass.

## 2. Target the orange group

Jail is the most visited space on the board, because so many cards and the Go to Jail corner send players there. The orange streets sit **6, 8 and 9 spaces** past Jail, and 6 and 8 are among the most common totals with two dice. That makes orange the most landed-on colour group. Houses there are also cheaper than on red, yellow or green, so the return is excellent.

## 3. Red is a close second

The red group, especially the third street, also gets plenty of traffic from players leaving jail and from Chance cards that send players there. If orange is taken, go for red.

## 4. Don't ignore the railroads

Four railroads pay $200 rent on every landing and need no buildings. They're spread around the board, so they get landed on steadily. They're a strong early investment and great for trades. **Utilities**, on the other hand, are the weakest buy in the game. Take them if they're cheap, but don't pay much.

## 5. Build to three houses as fast as you can

The biggest jump in rent comes with the **third house**. On most streets, three houses charge roughly two to three times the rent of two houses. Get every street in your set to three houses before spending on anything else.

## 6. Use the housing shortage

The bank has only 32 houses. If you build four houses on your streets instead of upgrading to hotels, those houses stay off the market, and your opponents may not be able to build at all. Late in the game, a housing shortage can win it for you.

## 7. Keep a cash cushion

Going all in on houses feels great until you land on a hotel and have to sell them back at half price. Keep enough cash to survive one or two bad landings. As a rough guide, hold at least $200 to $300 in the early game and more once hotels appear.

## 8. Trade to complete sets

Most games are won by whoever completes the first good colour group with buildings. If two players each hold a piece of the other's set, a trade helps both, but make sure it helps you more. Our [trading strategy guide](/monopoly-trading-strategy) covers how to make the deal work.

## 9. Know when to stay in jail

Early on, get out of jail quickly so you can keep buying. Later, when the board is full of houses, **staying in jail is a defence**. You still collect rent but can't land on anyone else's property. Read more in our [jail strategy](/monopoly-jail-strategy) guide.

## 10. Mortgage wisely

Mortgage the properties that matter least first: single streets from sets you can't complete, then utilities. Never mortgage a street in a group you're trying to build on, because you'd have to sell the houses first.

## Bonus: play the official rules

Many strategies change with house rules. Free Parking jackpots reward luck, and skipping auctions slows the game down. If you want skill to matter, try a game using the official rules.
