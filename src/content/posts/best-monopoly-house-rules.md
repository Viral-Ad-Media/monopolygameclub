Almost every family plays Monopoly with a few rules that aren't in the box. Some make the game more fun. Others are the reason a "quick game" lasts four hours. Here's how the most popular house rules change the game, so you can pick the ones that suit your table.

Before you start, it's worth knowing the [official Monopoly rules](/official-monopoly-rules-explained). It's surprising how many people have never played them.

## The Free Parking jackpot

**How it works:** Taxes and fines go into the middle of the board instead of to the bank. Whoever lands on Free Parking takes the pile. Some families seed it with $500 at the start.

**What it does:** It puts extra money into the game, so players go bankrupt more slowly, and the game gets **much longer**. It also rewards pure luck over good decisions.

**Verdict:** Fun with young children. Skip it if you want to finish before midnight.

## Double money on GO

**How it works:** Land exactly on GO and collect $400 instead of $200.

**What it does:** Like the jackpot, it adds money and makes the game longer, but by less.

**Verdict:** Harmless, but not much gained.

## No buying on the first lap

**How it works:** Nobody can buy property until they've gone around the board once.

**What it does:** It slows the start and spreads property more randomly.

**Verdict:** Skip it. It adds time and takes away early choices.

## No auctions

**How it works:** If you don't buy a property, it stays with the bank for the next person who lands there.

**What it does:** This is probably the most common house rule, often without people realising it. It makes the game **much slower**, because colour groups take ages to complete.

**Verdict:** Try playing with auctions instead. You'll be amazed how much faster the game is.

## No rent while in jail

**How it works:** Players in jail can't collect rent.

**What it does:** It punishes bad luck and takes away the late-game strategy of [hiding in jail](/monopoly-jail-strategy).

**Verdict:** Not recommended. It makes jail a pure penalty.

## Deal out the properties

**How it works:** Shuffle the title deeds and deal two or three to each player before the game starts.

**What it does:** Players reach complete sets and start trading much faster. This rule is in the official rulebook as the **short game**, which also lets you win by having the most money when the second player goes bankrupt.

**Verdict:** Highly recommended for shorter games.

## Time limit

**How it works:** Set a timer for 60 or 90 minutes. When it goes off, everyone adds up their cash, property values and buildings, and the richest player wins.

**Verdict:** The best fix for games that never end. Great for a weeknight.

## Immunity deals

**How it works:** Players can trade "no rent on my orange streets for the next three landings" as part of a deal.

**What it does:** It adds lots of negotiation and some chaos. It's not in the official rules, so agree before the game whether it's allowed.

**Verdict:** Great with players who love to haggle. See our [trading strategy](/monopoly-trading-strategy) for more.

## Our recommended house rule set

For a game that's fun and finishes in about 90 minutes:

1. Play with **official auctions**.
2. **Deal two properties** to each player at the start.
3. **No Free Parking jackpot.**
4. Set a **90-minute timer** as a backstop.
