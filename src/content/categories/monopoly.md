Monopoly is the property trading game that has been arguing families into the small hours since the 1930s. Players race around a 40-space board buying streets, railroads and utilities, then collect rent from anyone unlucky enough to land on them. The last player who isn't bankrupt wins.

## The board at a glance

The classic board has **28 properties** that you can buy: 22 streets split into eight colour groups, four railroads and two utilities. The rest of the spaces are corners (GO, Jail, Free Parking and Go to Jail), Chance and Community Chest card spaces, and two tax squares.

## A short history

The game started as **The Landlord's Game**, patented in 1904 by Lizzie Magie, a game designer who wanted to show how renting land concentrates wealth in a few hands. Homemade versions spread through the 1910s and 1920s, picking up the street names of Atlantic City along the way. Charles Darrow sold his version to Parker Brothers in 1935, and it became one of the best-selling board games ever made. Hasbro has owned it since buying Parker Brothers in 1991.
