Catan (originally *The Settlers of Catan*) is the game that brought modern European-style board games to the mainstream. Designed by Klaus Teuber and first published in 1995, it has players settle an island, collect resources and trade with each other to grow their roads, settlements and cities.

## What you need to know

- **Players:** 3 to 4 in the base game (5 to 6 with the extension)
- **Goal:** be the first to reach **10 victory points** on your turn
- **Game length:** usually 60 to 90 minutes

The island is made of 19 terrain hexes. Each produces one resource: hills give **brick**, forests give **lumber**, pastures give **wool**, fields give **grain** and mountains give **ore**. The desert produces nothing and is where the robber starts.

## Building costs

| Build | Cost | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Road | 1 brick, 1 lumber | 0 |
| Settlement | 1 brick, 1 lumber, 1 wool, 1 grain | 1 |
| City (upgrade) | 2 grain, 3 ore | 2 |
| Development card | 1 ore, 1 wool, 1 grain | varies |

**Longest Road** (at least five connected road segments) and **Largest Army** (at least three knights played) are each worth 2 bonus points.
