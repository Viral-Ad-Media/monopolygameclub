Stratego is a two-player battle game of hidden information. Each player commands an army of 40 pieces whose ranks face away from the opponent. You only find out what you're fighting when pieces clash, so memory and bluffing matter as much as tactics. The goal is to **capture the enemy flag**, or to leave your opponent with no pieces they can move.

## The board and pieces

The board is a 10 × 10 grid with two lakes in the middle that no piece can enter. Each player sets up all 40 pieces in the four rows closest to them, in any arrangement.

| Rank | Piece | Count | Special ability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | Marshal | 1 | Strongest piece, but loses to the Spy if the Spy attacks |
| 9 | General | 1 | |
| 8 | Colonel | 2 | |
| 7 | Major | 3 | |
| 6 | Captain | 4 | |
| 5 | Lieutenant | 4 | |
| 4 | Sergeant | 4 | |
| 3 | Miner | 5 | The only piece that can defuse bombs |
| 2 | Scout | 8 | Moves any distance in a straight line |
| 1 | Spy | 1 | Defeats the Marshal when it attacks |
| B | Bomb | 6 | Never moves, and destroys any attacker except a Miner |
| F | Flag | 1 | Never moves. Lose it and you lose the game |

Older European sets number the ranks the other way round, with 1 as the Marshal, but the pieces work the same.

## How to play

Players take turns moving one piece one square forwards, backwards or sideways (never diagonally). Scouts can move any number of empty squares in a straight line. To attack, move onto a square occupied by an enemy piece. Both players reveal the ranks, and the lower rank is removed. If the ranks are equal, both pieces are removed.

## Strategy tips

- **Guard your flag with bombs**, but don't make it obvious. A back-row corner surrounded by bombs is the first place experienced players look.
- **Use scouts to gather information.** They're cheap, and they reveal what the enemy is hiding.
- **Keep your Marshal hidden** until you know where the enemy Spy is.
- **Bluff.** Move a low piece boldly as if it were a high one, and leave a high piece still like a bomb.
- **Count what you've seen.** Once both enemy Miners are gone, your bombs are untouchable.
