Draughts, called checkers in North America, is one of the oldest board games still played today. Two players move pieces diagonally across a checkered board, capturing by jumping over enemy pieces. Reach the far side and your piece is crowned a king.

There are many national versions. **English draughts** is played on an 8 × 8 board with 12 pieces each. **International draughts**, popular in the Netherlands, France and West Africa, uses a 10 × 10 board, 20 pieces each and "flying" kings that move any distance.
