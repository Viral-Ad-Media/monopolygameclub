Battleship is a guessing game for two players. Each player secretly places a fleet of ships on a 10 × 10 grid, then the two take turns calling out coordinates to fire at. The first player to sink the whole enemy fleet wins.

It started as a pencil-and-paper game in the early 20th century. Milton Bradley released the familiar plastic version with pegs in 1967, and electronic editions followed.
